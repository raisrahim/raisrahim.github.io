I'm a software architect and engineering leader living in Jakarta, Indonesia. I lead the Global Development Center at Hyundai AutoEver Indonesia, where I work on enterprise platforms multi-region across APAC, Europe, and North America.

Over 15+ years, I've designed and built enterprise software across banking, insurance, fintech, and airport management. My work includes distributed systems, cloud architecture, authorization, and data pipelines. At Hyundai AutoEver, I also grew the software development team from 0 to 20+ engineers.

I engineered open-source projects for Hyundai Motor Group, including [hmg-Role](https://github.com/hyundaimotorgroup/hmg-role), a hybrid RBAC and ABAC authorization framework, and [hmg-IPMap](https://github.com/hyundaimotorgroup/hmg-ipmap), a geolocation data pipeline.

Previously, I led the transition to high-concurrency microservices at [Muamalat Bank](https://www.bankmuamalat.co.id/) and designed a Risk Management System at [Alami Sharia](https://alamisharia.co.id/). My career has also included airport management systems and global data synchronization.

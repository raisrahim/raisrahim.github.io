I'm a systems architect and hands-on engineer living in Jakarta, Indonesia. Currently, I lead the Global Development Center at Hyundai AutoEver Indonesia, where I split my time between core systems implementation, multi-region architecture (APAC, EU, NA), and growing high-velocity engineering teams.

Over 15+ years, I’ve designed, built, and shipped software across banking, fintech, insurance, and airport management. My engineering focus centers on distributed backend runtimes, local-first authorization engines, and sub-millisecond data pipelines. Alongside hands-on code delivery, I scaled our Indonesia engineering organization from 0 to 20+ developers.

I architected and implemented Hyundai Motor Group’s first two open-source enterprise systems: [hmg-Role](https://github.com/hyundaimotorgroup/hmg-role), an embedded hybrid RBAC/ABAC authorization framework, and [hmg-IPMap](https://github.com/hyundaimotorgroup/hmg-ipmap), a low-latency geolocation pipeline backed by Valkey.

Previously, I engineered the microservices core for [Muamalat Bank](https://www.bankmuamalat.co.id/) and designed the modular Risk Management System at [Alami Sharia](https://alamisharia.co.id/). I build software, design systems, and lead teams.

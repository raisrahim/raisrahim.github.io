Hello World! I'm a systems architect and engineering leader living in Jakarta, Indonesia. I am currently the Head of Global Development Center at Hyundai AutoEver Indonesia.

I lead projects that scale multi-region enterprise platforms across APAC, EU, and NA, and recently scaled our software development team from 0 to 18+ engineers. 

I engineered the first open-sourced enterprise projects for Hyundai Motor Group, including **hmg-Role**, a decentralized authorization engine, and **hmg-IPMap**, a sub-millisecond geolocation pipeline. 

Previously, I orchestrated the transition to high-concurrency microservices at Bank Muamalat and designed the Risk Management System from scratch at Hijra Group. Over the past 15+ years, I have engineered enterprise software across multiple industries, including banking, insurance, fintech, and airport management.

This site serves as my digital archive and professional portfolio. Here, you will find my writing on software engineering and technology, alongside a curated selection of projects. I share my work here hoping it might be useful to others and spark new collaborations.
